About

I have spent two decades as a researcher and research manager, creating AI/ML and data-analytics technologies that solve critical business problems in running one of the world's largest networks.

At AT&T Labs – Research, I lead work that turns operational data from cellular and IP networks into practical tools. These tools assess the impact of network changes and upgrades, find the root cause of service problems, detect anomalies, and recommend configurations for LTE and 5G radio networks. I use design thinking to reach practical, effective solutions, and most of my inventions have been built into software systems deployed in real business operations. The research has been published at venues including ACM SIGCOMM, MobiCom, IMC, CoNEXT, SIGMETRICS, and IEEE INFOCOM.

I am an inventor or co-inventor on over 50 US patents (see list). I am a skilled full-stack software developer, and I manage a team of creative researchers who work together to deliver strong business results. I also enjoy mentoring research interns and working with academic collaborators.

Research Areas

Cellular configuration intelligence

Learning from network context to recommend and tune LTE/5G configurations, even when confounding factors are present.

Iridescence · Chroma · Aurora · Auric

Change & upgrade impact assessment

Rigorous, statistically sound ways to tell whether software upgrades, configuration changes, and maintenance help or hurt service.

Mercury · Litmus · Magus · Reflection

Service quality & root cause analysis

Platforms that detect end-to-end service problems and diagnose them across large IP, IPTV, and mobile networks.

G-RCA · Argus · Giza · ABSENCE

Network measurement & traffic analysis

Traffic matrix estimation, network anomography, data sketching, and mining large volumes of syslog and management data.

Anomography · Crossroads · NetSearch
